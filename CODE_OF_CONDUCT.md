# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
v2.1.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer via
the contact listed in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

For the full text of the Contributor Covenant, see
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.
